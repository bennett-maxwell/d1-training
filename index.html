Franchise Opportunity Profile

D1 Training

Athletic-based scholastic and adult group training, modeled on Division 1 strength and conditioning principles. Every fact on this page is sourced from the D1 Training 2025 Franchise Disclosure Document (amended August 25, 2025; as of September 16, 2025), with Item and page citations.

Key Facts

Initial Franchise Fee

$59,500

Uniformly charged. 2nd unit $40,000; 3rd $35,000; 4th+ $30,000. Non-refundable. Veterans: 10% off. (FDD Item 5, p.10-11)

Estimated Initial Investment

$480,557 to $933,432

Average prototype investment $701,143. Affiliated-fee total approx $163,000. (FDD Item 7, p.17-18)

Royalty

7% of Gross Sales

Greater of 7% of Gross Sales or minimum royalty: $1,950/mo yr 1, $2,450/mo yr 2, $2,950/mo yr 3+. (FDD Item 6, p.12)

Brand Fund

2% of Gross Sales

Greater of $250/mo or 2% of monthly Gross Sales. (FDD Item 6, p.13)

Local Marketing

$100 per day

Subject to 10-20% annual increases. (FDD Item 6, p.13)

Territory

Non-exclusive

0.20 to 3-mile radius around the Premises, based on household density. Site selection area ~7,500-20,000 households. (FDD Item 12, p.34)

Term

10 years + 10-year renewal

One successive 10-year term; renewal fee $15,000. Post-term non-compete: 2 years, 10-mile radius. (FDD Item 17, pp.41-46)

Facility

4,000 to 5,000 sq ft

Average go-forward site ~4,300 sq ft; minimum 7 squat racks. Franchisor site/lease approval required. (FDD Item 1, p.4; Item 7, p.18)

Training

~1 week at HQ + on-site grand opening

Franchisee and GM attend; online Operations Manuals; MindBody/Scorpion/ProfitKeeper tech stack. (FDD Item 11, p.26)

Time to Open

Within 365 days

Site approval within 150 days; lease approval within 180 days of signing. (FDD Item 11, p.26)

HQ

Franklin, Tennessee

7115 S. Springs Drive, Franklin, TN 37067. (615) 933-5653. (FDD Item 1, p.1)

System Size

129 outlets

129 total outlets at 12/31/2024 (127 franchised + 2 company-owned). (FDD Item 20 Table No. 1, p.53)
